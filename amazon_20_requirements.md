# Scenario: Amazon Full E-Commerce Shopping Journey (20 Requirements)

## Requirements
1. Navigate to https://www.amazon.in.
2. Verify home page logo is visible.
3. Type "wireless headphones" into search textbox.
4. Click search submit button.
5. Verify search results list is visible.
6. Click first product item in search results.
7. Verify product title detail page is visible.
8. Verify product price is displayed.
9. Check customer ratings and review count visibility.
10. Check product availability status.
11. Click Add to Cart button.
12. Verify item added to cart confirmation modal.
13. Click Go to Cart link.
14. Verify shopping cart page contains selected item.
15. Verify item subtotal price is displayed.
16. Click Proceed to Checkout button.
17. Verify sign-in prompt page is displayed.
18. Enter customer email or phone number.
19. Click Continue button.
20. Verify password entry field or verification screen is visible.
